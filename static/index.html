Read-only review. Mizan never runs submitted code, exploits, or reproduction steps and never contacts a reported target. Cases are scoped to a workspace; reports and snippets are stored as plaintext in the local SQLite database. Each researcher's Meta key is encrypted at rest and is sent with report content only after that researcher explicitly confirms analysis. Recommendations are not final decisions.
